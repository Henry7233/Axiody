Help Centre

General guides & FAQs

How do I get started?

Sign in with your own account. Review your account information, choose a comfortable theme and text size, then read the guide for your role below.

Where are my settings saved?

Theme and font size are saved to your signed-in account and apply across all pages and devices. Other preferences are stored in this browser for your account.

What do Save Changes and Close do?

Save Changes validates and saves the account form. Close discards edits made since the last save and returns to the account summary. Appearance and notification choices save immediately.

Client guides & FAQs

How do I submit documents?

Open Submit, enter a title and the document date, add a description if helpful, then choose your files. Check the selected files before submitting. Use Add + for another document entry.

What if my document needs changes?

Read the review feedback, correct the specific issue and provide a complete, readable copy. Check the requested reporting period and deadline. Contact support if the request is unclear.
