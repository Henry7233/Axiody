Understanding your notifications

Results appear after document classification and information validation have both completed.

  • Successful: your documents contain the required information.
  • Unsuccessful: check the reason, affected filenames, and required changes.
  • Deadline: complete any changes by 11:59 pm SGT on the date shown. Otherwise, your submission will be postponed to the next review cycle.
